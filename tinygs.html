Featured project · Satellite communications

WiridLAB_UMNG_V2: satellite signals from Cajicá.

A TinyGS ground station used to observe real satellite receptions and bring telemetry, propagation and orbital passes into the laboratory.

View the live station on TinyGS ↗

From received packets to engineering questions.

WiridLAB_UMNG_V2 participates in the TinyGS network from Cajicá, Colombia. The public station page shows its current reception record and available packet details; these values change over time.

We use the station as a source of real observations for students to compare satellite passes, received signal strength (RSSI), signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) and packet reception. The same observations can support questions about antenna placement, coverage and ground-station diversity.

Learning with real data

Questions for a satellite communications class.

When is a satellite visible?

Compare predicted passes from orbital elements with the times of actual receptions.

How strong is the signal?

Examine RSSI and SNR across packets and consider the effects of geometry and the radio link.

What changes between stations?

Compare reception records when observations from other ground stations are available.
