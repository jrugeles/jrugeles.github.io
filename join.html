Student opportunities · Wirid-LAB

Join a project and learn by building.

We welcome students interested in internships, final-year projects and research activities in wireless communications. Tell us what you would like to work on, and we can identify a suitable opportunity.

What can you work on?

Projects combine hands-on experiments, reproducible measurements and documentation. Current areas include satellite reception with TinyGS, software-defined radio, IoT and embedded telemetry, wireless security, and LoRa or Meshtastic networks.

  • Satellite communicationsGround stations, packet analysis, orbital passes and link measurements.
  • SDR and RFGNU Radio, instrumentation, signal processing and wireless security.
  • IoT and mesh networksSensing nodes, telemetry, radio links and field testing.

Experience helps, but curiosity, care with measurements and willingness to document your work matter too.
