WiridLab

Wireless and Radio Innovation Laboratory.

An experimental laboratory environment for software-defined radio, wireless communications, satellite reception, embedded systems and remote-access engineering education.

One laboratory, multiple radio platforms.

WiridLab brings together SDR hardware, embedded wireless nodes, satellite ground-station systems and network infrastructure so experiments can move from bench measurements to remote and field deployments.
