About

Professor, researcher and experimental telecommunications educator.

My academic work focuses on bringing wireless communications theory into direct contact with signals, instruments, software-defined radio and deployable systems.

Academic profile

José de Jesús Rugeles Uribe, Ph.D., is a professor in Telecommunications Engineering at Universidad Militar Nueva Granada (UMNG). His work spans software-defined radio, wireless and IoT security, RF experimentation, satellite communications and engineering education.

He currently serves as Director of the Telecommunications Engineering Program at UMNG, while developing hands-on laboratory initiatives that connect research infrastructure with student learning.

My teaching philosophy is simple: students understand communications more deeply when they can generate, observe, measure and explain real signals.
